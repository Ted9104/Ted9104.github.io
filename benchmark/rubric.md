# Scoring rubric

Five criteria, each scored 1 to 5, total out of 25. A 2 or 4 means the image sits between the anchors. Same text as the [methodology page](https://ted9104.github.io/methodology.html#scoring).

| Criterion | What is checked | 1 | 3 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Product fidelity | Shape, proportions, cap, bulb and pipette match the input | Different product, or a key part missing or reshaped | Clearly the same bottle, one detail changed (for example cap ribs or pipette missing) | Indistinguishable from the input bottle |
| Text and marks | No unrequested text, label or logo. For prompt 8: requested text spelled exactly and wrapped on the glass | Invented branding, or prompt 8 text misspelled | Small stray mark, or prompt 8 text correct but flat or warped | Clean glass, or prompt 8 label correct and natural |
| Prompt adherence | Scene, props, light direction and aspect ratio as written | Most of the prompt ignored | Main scene right, one or two elements wrong or missing | Everything asked for is present |
| Realism | Light, shadow, reflections, glass transparency, contact with the surface | Obviously artificial: floating product, wrong shadows | Believable at a glance, flaws visible on a closer look | Reads as a real photograph at full size |
| Store-ready | Could go on a product page or ad without retouching | Unusable | Usable after light retouching | Usable as delivered |

Tools that take no written prompt (ModoAI, Pomelli) are scored on fidelity, realism and store-readiness only.
