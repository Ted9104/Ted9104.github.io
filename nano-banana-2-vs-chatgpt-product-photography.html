Nano Banana 2 or ChatGPT for product photography?

On the same product photo and the same 10 prompts, ChatGPT Free kept the real product in all 10 runs (fidelity 4 of 5), while Nano Banana 2 redrew it as a generic bottle in all 10 (fidelity 2 of 5). Nano Banana 2 was faster and spelled label text better. Use ChatGPT when the product must stay exact.

How is this comparison different from other Nano Banana 2 vs ChatGPT tests?

Many published comparisons of ChatGPT Images and Nano Banana 2 score pictures generated from text prompts: which image looks better. A seller's question is different: does the tool keep my actual product? Here both tools got the same uploaded photo of a real product, and the main score is product fidelity, whether the bottle in the output is still the same bottle. That is where the two models differed most.

When should you use each one?

  • Your product has a distinctive shape, cap or packaging: ChatGPT. Nano Banana 2 swapped our bottle every time.
  • You need readable text in the scene, or many quick drafts: Nano Banana 2.
  • You do not know what scene to ask for: neither tool plans it for you; see AI creative director tools, which decide the concept from your photo.

How do you keep your product unchanged in either tool?

Name the parts that must not change ("keep the black ribbed cap, the wide glass pipette and the amber color exactly as in the photo"), keep one product per image, and compare the first result against your photo before editing. In our runs the generic line "keep the bottle exactly as in the photo" did not stop Nano Banana 2 from redrawing it.

FAQ

Is Nano Banana 2 better than ChatGPT for product photos?

Not when the product must stay exact. In our 10-prompt test ChatGPT Free scored 4 of 5 on product fidelity every time and Nano Banana 2 scored 2. Nano Banana 2 was faster and better at label text.

Is ChatGPT free enough for product photography?

For a small batch. Our ChatGPT Free account hit its image limit after the 10th image on 2026-10-27.

Why did Nano Banana 2 change my product?

In our runs it rebuilt the bottle as a generic dropper bottle with different proportions, even with a preserve line in every prompt. Name the specific parts to keep and check each result against your photo.
