How were the four tools tested?

Every tool received the same 1600 × 2400 px photo of an unlabeled amber glass dropper bottle with a black ribbed cap, a black bulb and a glass pipette. Glass, reflections and small parts show distortion quickly, and with no brand marks on the bottle, any text in an output was invented by the tool.

  • ChatGPT and Nano Banana 2: the same 10 written prompts, pasted word for word, one new chat per prompt. Only the first image was scored; no rerolls, no edits.
  • Pomelli and ModoAI: each tool's normal flow. Pomelli built six shots from templates it pre-selected; ModoAI recommended a style, proposed three concepts, and we generated its first pick.
  • Scoring: 1 to 5 on product fidelity, text and marks, prompt adherence, realism and store-readiness. The anchors for each score are on the methodology page.

Which AI tool keeps your product exactly the same?

ChatGPT Free did, in our test. It kept the tall cap, the wide pipette and the small bubbles near the base in all 10 runs and scored 4 of 5 on fidelity every time. Nano Banana 2 rebuilt the bottle as a generic dropper bottle in all 10 runs (2 of 5). Pomelli and ModoAI each scored 3 of 5 in one run.

Which one makes the most realistic product photos?

Google Pomelli Photoshoot, in our one session. Its Studio template scored 5 of 5 on realism and 4 of 5 on store-readiness. ChatGPT, Nano Banana 2 and ModoAI all scored 4 on realism. Pomelli's result is a single session, so treat it as one data point, not a ranking.

Which is fastest, and which handles text on the product?

Nano Banana 2 on both counts. It returned a first image in 15 to 21 seconds, against 25 to 33 seconds for ChatGPT to a finished image (timed to slightly different end points). When we asked for a label reading ROSEHIP FACE OIL, Nano Banana 2 scored 4 of 5 on text and ChatGPT 3 of 5. Both spelled it correctly.

ModoAI does not create text or logos on products (what ModoAI does not do). If your listing needs printed text in the image, use a general image model or add text afterward.

Which AI tool plans the photoshoot for you?

ModoAI. From one photo and no prompt, it recommended a "Natural" style, explained that wood textures and leaf shadows would tie the amber bottle to botanical ingredients, proposed three concepts and generated its first pick in about 70 seconds. Pomelli also needs no prompt, but it applies templates it pre-selects rather than recommending one style with a stated reason.

With ChatGPT and Nano Banana 2 you decide the scene and write it out. Both followed written scenes well (prompt adherence 5 of 5 in 9 of 10 ChatGPT runs and 7 of 10 Nano Banana 2 runs), so they suit sellers who already know the shot they want. Which AI tool should you use for your product photos?

  • Your product has a distinctive shape, cap or packaging, and you can write a prompt: ChatGPT. It kept our bottle best.
  • You want many photo-real lifestyle shots for free, including people: Google Pomelli Photoshoot.
  • You need readable text in the image, or fast drafts: Nano Banana 2. Check the product shape in every result.
  • You have a product photo but no idea for the scene, and cannot use people in your images: ModoAI. It plans the style and concepts and explains why. It does not generate people, faces or body parts (policy).
  • You need background removal or batch edits across a catalog: none of these four was tested for that. Dedicated editors such as Photoroom are covered in our next round.

What are the limits of this test?

  • One product. A glass bottle is hard for image models; fabric, food or matte packaging may score differently.
  • Unequal runs. ChatGPT and Nano Banana 2 were scored on 10 runs each; Pomelli and ModoAI on one each. A second run could score higher or lower.
  • Different flows. Pomelli and ModoAI were not given our prompts, so only fidelity, realism and store-readiness are compared across all four.
  • Different accounts. Nano Banana 2 ran on a Google Workspace business account; ChatGPT on the Free plan.
  • One scorer. Ted scored every image against the written rubric. Scores are a documented judgment, not a lab measurement.
  • Location. Runs were made from South Korea in English. Models and limits can differ by country and account.

FAQ

Is ChatGPT good for product photos?

Yes, if you can describe the scene. In our 10-prompt test, ChatGPT Free kept the product's shape in every run (fidelity 4 of 5) and scored 22 of 25 on 9 runs. Its weak spot was printed text, and the free plan hit its image limit after the 10th image.

Which AI model is best for product photography?

For keeping the product unchanged, ChatGPT's image model did best in our test. For speed and text in the image, Nano Banana 2 did better. Image models return a different image on every run, so check each result against your original.

Is there a free AI tool for product photos?

Yes. Google Pomelli Photoshoot is described by Google as free; ChatGPT and the Gemini app include image creation on free plans with usage limits; ModoAI gives 20 free credits for 7 days without a card. Limits change, so check each official page.

Which AI tool won't change my product?

None kept our bottle identical. ChatGPT came closest (4 of 5 in 10 of 10 runs). Pomelli and ModoAI scored 3 of 5 in one run each, and Nano Banana 2 scored 2 of 5 in all 10 runs. Naming the parts that must not change in your prompt helps with ChatGPT and Nano Banana.

Can AI product photo tools add models or hands?

Some can. ChatGPT and Nano Banana 2 both produced a hand holding the bottle in our test, and Pomelli's "In Use" template showed a person. ModoAI does not generate people, faces or body parts, including on-model shots.
