Who runs this: Ted. Ted works at Uneedcomms, the company that makes ModoAI (Modo AI), a paid tool in the same category. This page tests only Pomelli. How tests are run.

Does Pomelli Photoshoot keep your product the same?

Pomelli Photoshoot is a free Google Labs tool that turns one product photo into a set of styled marketing shots. We ran it on 3 real product photos (stock photos from Unsplash) on 2026-11-03, with its 6 default templates each and no rerolls. 10 of the 18 shots scored 4 or 5 out of 5 for keeping the product the same.

For each product, the shot where a person holds it was the lowest or tied for lowest (2, 3 and 3; two other amber shots also scored 3). On each product, the shots without a person or hand averaged higher fidelity than the held shot (3.5, 4.2 and 3.6 vs 2, 3 and 3). Check proportions on shots with a person before using them.

How we tested

  • Tool: Google Pomelli (Google Labs, free), Photoshoot flow. No model name is shown in the app, so we do not name one.
  • Inputs: 3 stock photos from Unsplash: a peanut butter jar with a blank label, a perfume bottle with a printed label, and an amber dropper bottle with no label.
  • Settings: the 6 templates Pomelli pre-selected for each product, kept as is; default 9:16 output; no prompt; no rerolls.
  • Timing: the jar set ran at about 13:30 KST on 2026-11-03. Pomelli did not save the perfume and amber sets from that session, so we ran them again at about 17:30 KST the same day with the same settings. Those second runs are the ones shown.
  • Scoring: each full-resolution file was compared side by side with its input photo.

Limits

  • 3 products, 1 run each, 1 scorer. These are observations on these 3 products only.
  • Free plan only.
  • All three products are small bottles or jars. Clothing and large items were not tested.

Earlier run (2026-10-28)

A week earlier we ran Pomelli once on the amber dropper bottle alone. Its studio shot scored fidelity 3, realism 5, store-ready 4: the glass pipette was drawn thinner than in the photo. We keep that run separate and do not add it to the 18 shots above.

FAQ

Is Pomelli Photoshoot free?

Google describes Pomelli as a free tool from Google Labs. Our runs on 2026-10-28 and 2026-11-03 did not ask for payment.

Does Pomelli Photoshoot keep my product the same?

Mostly, on these 3 products. 10 of 18 shots scored 4 or 5 of 5 for product fidelity, 7 scored 3 and 1 scored 2. On the jar, the black lid and blank label were kept in all 6. On the perfume, the printed label was kept in 5 of 6. The jar was drawn narrower in 4 of 6; the amber bottle went the other way in 2 shots (wider or broader).

Can Pomelli Photoshoot keep small label text readable?

On our perfume bottle, WHITE ORCHID, EAU DE PARFUM and 100ml were readable in the white studio shot. In the shot where a model holds the bottle, the small text was not readable.

Which Pomelli shots changed the product most?

In our test, the shots where a person holds or uses the product: the peanut butter jar scored 2 (much smaller and narrower), the perfume bottle 3 (its only score below 4) and the amber bottle 3, tied with two other amber shots.

Do I need to write a prompt?

No. Pomelli picked 6 templates for each product and we kept them without editing.
