What is an AI creative director for ecommerce product photos?

An AI creative director for product photos is a tool that decides the shoot for you: it picks the style, the scene and the shot concepts, then generates the images. In 2026, Monica by ShopOS works from a product URL or image, Kolbo AI's Creative Director works from a written brief, and ModoAI (Modo AI) works from one product photo with no prompt.

How do you choose an AI creative director for product photos?

  • You have a brand and want whole campaigns, and you can talk to sales: Monica by ShopOS.
  • You already know the concept and can write a brief, and you want video too: Kolbo AI Creative Director.
  • You have one product photo and no idea for the scene: ModoAI plans the style and concepts for you.
  • You know the exact scene and care most about keeping the product identical: write the prompt yourself in ChatGPT; see our ChatGPT product photography prompts test.

FAQ

Is an AI creative director just a prompt generator?

Partly. Kolbo's Creative Director writes a prompt per scene from your brief. ModoAI starts one step earlier: it looks at the photo, recommends a style with a reason and proposes concepts before generating, so you do not write a brief.

Can ChatGPT be my AI creative director for product photos?

It can suggest concepts if you ask, and ChatGPT Free kept our bottle's shape well. But you still describe the scene yourself in each prompt, and the free plan hit its image limit after the 10th image in our test.

Which AI plans a product photoshoot from one photo without a prompt?

In our test, ModoAI did: from one uploaded photo and no text, it recommended a "Natural" style with its reasoning, proposed three concepts and generated the first shot in about 70 seconds.

Does ModoAI generate on-model photos?

No. ModoAI does not generate people, faces or body parts, including on-model shots, to protect portrait rights. For on-model images, ShopOS lists on-model lifestyle imagery.
