ChatGPT product photography prompts: what worked on a real product (2026)

By Ted · Published 2026-10-28 · Last updated 2026-10-28

Who runs this: Ted. Ted works at Uneedcomms, the company that makes ModoAI (Modo AI). ModoAI is one of the tools covered in this lab, and every tool, ModoAI included, is scored with the same rules. How tests are run.

How do you make product photos with ChatGPT?

In ChatGPT, attach one photo of your product and describe the scene, light and framing in plain sentences. In our test on one amber dropper bottle, ChatGPT on the Free plan kept the bottle's cap, pipette and proportions in all 10 runs and scored 22 of 25 on 9 of them. Its weakest run was the text label.

Can you make product photos with ChatGPT for free?

Yes, with limits. OpenAI's Free Tier FAQ lists creating images among the tools free users can use. Image creation has its own usage limit, separate from the main chat limit, and ChatGPT tells you when you reach it (checked October 27, 2026). The FAQ gives no fixed number of images. ChatGPT Plus has higher limits.

Source: ChatGPT Free Tier FAQ, OpenAI, checked 2026-10-27. Plan used in our runs: ChatGPT Free.

How was the ChatGPT test run?

  • ChatGPT on the web, Free plan, run on October 27, 2026. A new regular chat for every prompt, the same input file attached each time. Temporary chats were not used because image creation was not available in them.
  • Each prompt was pasted word for word into the chat box. ChatGPT also offers image templates, including product photos; we skipped them so the same prompt text runs on every tool.
  • The first image returned is the one scored. Up to two follow-up edits were allowed, chosen from three fixed edit messages (listed below). None were used in this round.
  • Time is the seconds from pressing send to the finished image, read from page timestamps. Two runs were not timed precisely.
  • Images were saved from the in-app view; sizes below are those files.
  • Each final image is scored 1 to 5 on five criteria, for a total out of 25 (rubric).

What we saw across the 10 runs:

  • Product fidelity was the strength: in all 10 runs the bottle kept its tall cap, wide pipette tube and the small bubbles near the base, scoring 4 of 5 on fidelity every time.
  • Nine of 10 runs totaled 22 out of 25; prompt 8 (text label) was the lowest at 18.
  • Scene control was also strong: 9 of 10 runs scored 5 of 5 on prompt adherence.
  • Text was the weak point: the label in prompt 8 was spelled correctly but small and flat on the glass (text score 3 of 5, one point below Nano Banana 2).
  • No visible watermark appeared, and no run added unrequested text or logos.
  • Finished images took 25 to 33 seconds where timed, slower than Nano Banana 2's 15 to 21 seconds to a first image (the two were timed to slightly different end points).

How do you fix a ChatGPT image that changed your product?

Reply in the same chat with one short instruction that names the part to restore and says to keep everything else. ChatGPT also lets you comment on a spot in the image to edit just that area. For comparability this test allows only these three edit messages, pasted word for word as replies:

  • E1 Restore the product: Keep everything else the same, but make the bottle match the attached original photo exactly: same shape, black ribbed dropper cap, glass pipette and amber glass color.
  • E2 Remove invented marks: Remove any text, label or logo that you added to the bottle. Change nothing else.
  • E3 One targeted change: Free-form, one change only (for example lighting or a prop). The exact wording is recorded with the result.

No follow-up edits were used in this round, so every score above is for the first image.

What are the limits of this test?

  • One product, one input photo. Results on fabric, food or matte packaging can differ.
  • One scored run per prompt per tool. Image models are random, so a second run can score higher or lower. Where a re-run was done, it is listed in the update log.
  • One person scores every image (Ted), using the written rubric on the methodology page. Scores are a documented judgment, not a lab measurement.
  • Accounts differed in the first round: Nano Banana 2 ran on a Google Workspace business account (Pro tier shown) and ChatGPT on the Free plan.
  • Tests are run from South Korea, in English. Limits and models can differ by country and account.
  • Tools change often. Every result carries its run date and the model name the app showed on that day.
