What is Product Photo Test Lab?

Product Photo Test Lab runs one real product photo through popular AI image tools and publishes everything: the exact prompts, the unretouched outputs, the time each run took, the edits used and a 25-point score. It is for online sellers deciding how to make product photos with AI, and it is run by Ted.

First round (2026-10-27): on the same 10 prompts, ChatGPT Free kept the bottle's shape in all 10 runs (fidelity 4 of 5, 9 of 10 runs at 22 of 25), while Nano Banana 2 redrew it as a generic dropper bottle in all 10 (fidelity 2 of 5, totals 16 to 18). Nano Banana 2 spelled the text label better and returned images faster. ModoAI, given the same photo and no prompt, planned the style and concepts itself and scored 3 of 5 on fidelity (details).

Why test AI product photography on one photo?

A prompt only helps a seller if the tool keeps the actual product intact: its shape, color, small parts and labels. Using one photo for every tool makes the results comparable. Publishing the first image returned, plus every edit, shows how much work a usable shot takes, including the runs that failed.

How are results scored?

Each final image gets 1 to 5 points on five criteria: product fidelity, text and marks, prompt adherence, realism and store-readiness. The total is out of 25. Every run also records the date, plan, model name, seconds to the first image and edits used. The anchors for each score are on the methodology page.

Who runs this lab, and what is the conflict of interest?

Ted runs Product Photo Test Lab as a personal site. Ted works at Uneedcomms, the company that makes ModoAI (Modo AI), an AI product photography tool that is included in these tests. To keep that interest from shaping results, every tool gets the same photo, prompts and rubric, competitor facts come only from official pages with dates, and pages state where other tools beat ModoAI.

How often are the tests updated?

Every four weeks, and within two weeks of a tool releasing a new image model. Each page shows its last updated date and keeps an update log.
