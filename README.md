# Product Photo Test Lab

The lab puts the same real product photo and the same prompts into each AI image tool, then publishes every output and score. Site: https://ted9104.github.io

Run by Ted, who works at Uneedcomms, the company that makes ModoAI (Modo AI). ModoAI is one of the tools tested, and the pages say where other tools did better.

## Tests

- [How to use Nano Banana for product photography](https://ted9104.github.io/nano-banana-2-product-photography.html): 5 steps and 10 prompts on a real product. The bottle was redrawn in 10 of 10 runs, and a restore request fixed 0 of 10.
- [ChatGPT product photography prompts](https://ted9104.github.io/chatgpt-product-photography-prompts.html): 10 prompts on ChatGPT Free. The bottle kept its shape in 10 of 10 runs (fidelity 4 of 5).
- [ChatGPT vs Nano Banana for product photography](https://ted9104.github.io/nano-banana-2-vs-chatgpt-product-photography.html): the same photo and prompts, compared side by side.
- [Best AI tool for product photos](https://ted9104.github.io/best-ai-tool-for-product-photos.html): one product run through four tools.
- [Methodology](https://ted9104.github.io/methodology.html): rubric, fields, fairness rules.
