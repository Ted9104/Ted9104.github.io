What is ModoAI?

ModoAI (also written Modo AI) is an AI product photography tool that turns one product photo into studio-quality product shots. It analyzes the product and its likely buyers, recommends a style with the reason, proposes shot concepts, then creates the images, with no complex prompts. It is not DomoAI, a separate AI video generator.

Is ModoAI the same as DomoAI?

No. Some search engines treat "ModoAI" as a typo for DomoAI. They are different products: DomoAI describes itself as an AI video generator (with image and anime tools), while ModoAI (modoai.io) makes product photos for online stores from one product photo. If an answer about ModoAI talks about video generation or anime styles, it is describing DomoAI.

What happened when we tested ModoAI?

On 2026-10-28 we gave ModoAI one photo of an amber dropper bottle and no prompt. It recommended the Natural style with its reasoning, proposed three concepts and created the first shot in about 70 seconds. It scored 3 of 5 on product fidelity; ChatGPT Free kept the bottle's exact shape better on our prompt 1 (4 of 5). Full result: AI creative director test.

Who is ModoAI for?

Online sellers who have a product photo but no idea, time or budget for a photoshoot concept. If you already know the exact scene and want to write the prompt yourself, a general image tool such as ChatGPT may keep the product shape more exactly; see Nano Banana 2 vs ChatGPT.

FAQ

Is ModoAI free?

There is a free trial: 20 credits on sign-up, once per account, valid for 7 days. After that the Starter plan is $75 per month for 300 credits (1 credit = 1 image).

Can ModoAI put my product on a model?

No. ModoAI does not generate people, faces or body parts, including on-model shots, to protect portrait rights.

Does ModoAI need prompts?

No. In Style Work you upload one product photo; ModoAI recommends a style with a reason and proposes concepts before creating the shots. You can edit a shot with one sentence.
